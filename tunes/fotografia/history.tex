\begin{notes}[History]
Bossa nova song written in 1959 by Antônio Carlos Jobim, one of the first for which he
wrote the words as well as the music. English lyrics (``Photograph'') by Ray Gilbert,
published in 1965.
\sources{\src{Wikipedia, ``Fotografia (Antônio Carlos Jobim song)''}{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fotografia_(Ant\%C3\%B4nio_Carlos_Jobim_song)}.}
\end{notes}
